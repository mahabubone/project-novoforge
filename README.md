# Novoforge

**Local-first worldbuilding and novel-writing software.** No accounts, no
cloud, no lock-in — your project is a folder on disk, and everything
(database, assets, revisions, backups) lives right next to it. Your writing
never leaves your machine unless you choose to back it up somewhere.

Built for people who want a quiet place to write and build worlds, and to
keep full ownership of their work.

## Features

- **Manuscript** — a distraction-free editor with focus mode, typewriter
  scrolling, scrivenings view, and per-scene word targets.
- **Worldbuilding** — characters, locations, factions and more, with custom
  attributes, relationship graphs, timelines, calendars, maps and family
  trees.
- **History & safety** — automatic revision snapshots with a diff viewer and
  one-click restore, plus scheduled encrypted backups to a local folder or
  your Dropbox.
- **Export** — DOCX, EPUB, PDF, Markdown and more, with checksums so you can
  verify nothing was lost.
- **Stats** — words per day, streaks, chapter lengths, and writing targets.

## Download

Grab the latest version for your system from the
**[releases page](https://github.com/mahabubone/project-novoforge/releases)**:

| You are running… | Download |
| --- | --- |
| **Windows** | `Novoforge-<version>-setup.exe` |
| **Linux** (Debian/Ubuntu) | `Novoforge-<version>.deb` |
| **Linux** (Fedora/RHEL) | `Novoforge-<version>.rpm` |
| **Linux** (any distro) | `Novoforge-<version>.AppImage` |
| **macOS** | `Novoforge-<version>-mac.dmg` |

The first run asks you to create or open a project folder. That folder *is*
your project — keep it backed up and you keep your novel.

## Updates

Novoforge checks for updates automatically and installs them in place. On
Linux you can also just download the new AppImage and run it.
